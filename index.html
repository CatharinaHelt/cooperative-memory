Cooperative environmental memory

A cooperative short-term/long-term memory for autonomous robots that shares environmental knowledge and revises it when evidence changes.

Catharina Helten · RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau

The problem

Current perception is local. Useful knowledge should not be.

A robot can miss an obstacle because of occlusion, noise, or a temporary sensor failure.

At the same time, a previously observed obstacle may have genuinely been removed.

The memory must therefore remain stable under short-lived errors while adapting to persistent environmental change.

About the author

Catharina Helten

PhD researcher · Adaptive Autonomy and Off-Road Robotics Lab · RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau

I work on adaptive autonomy for cooperative robotic systems, with a focus on environmental memory, uncertainty-aware knowledge sharing, and reliable operation in challenging off-road environments.
