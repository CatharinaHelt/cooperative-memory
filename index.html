The problem

Current perception is local. Useful knowledge should not be.

A robot can miss an obstacle because of occlusion, noise, or a temporary sensor failure. At the same time, a previously observed obstacle may have genuinely been removed.

The memory must therefore remain stable under short-lived errors while adapting to persistent environmental change.

This page collects the system overview, experimental protocol, quantitative results, implementation material, citation, and author information.

About the author

Catharina Helten

PhD researcher · Adaptive Autonomy and Off-Road Robotics Lab · RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau

I work on adaptive autonomy for cooperative robotic systems, with a focus on environmental memory, uncertainty-aware knowledge sharing, and reliable operation in challenging off-road environments.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a safety engineer at Fraunhofer IESE. My research combines robotics, environmental abstraction, and safety-oriented evaluation to make autonomous systems more aware of what they have observed — and what may have changed.
